What a small business website actually costs.

At Nimbus, a single-page website costs $350. A starter website costs $750 for up to three pages, and a larger business website costs $1,500 for up to five. These are one-time build prices with optional monthly help and separately agreed third-party costs.

See the complete Nimbus pricing and package details

A useful price comes with a clear scope. Page count, writing, photography, revisions and integrations all affect what you are buying.

These are Nimbus packages, not a claim about the average market price. Our pricing page gives the full inclusions and limits; we confirm your scope and schedule in writing before a deposit.

Start with the size of the job

The $350 single-page option covers up to five sections, existing branding, 350 words of supplied copy lightly edited, three supplied photos and one revision round. It includes call and email links instead of a contact form. It is a small online presence, with no custom integrations or migration included.

The $750 starter covers up to three pages using two layouts, 750 words based on your business details, ten supplied photos, one contact form, one consolidated revision round and up to three simple redirects. It suits a local business that needs a focused first site.

The $1,500 business website covers up to five pages using three layouts, 1,500 words, twenty supplied photos, one contact form, two consolidated revision rounds and up to ten simple redirects. It provides more room to explain separate services and show real work.

Both cover one business and one language, mobile-friendly design, basic search setup and a checked contact form. Stores, booking systems, extra pages, photography and larger migrations need a separate quote.

Separate the build from monthly costs

Website care is optional at $75 per month for a Nimbus-built static site. It includes uptime alerts, a monthly contact check, routine dependency maintenance and up to 30 minutes of small text or photo changes. Unused edit time does not roll over.

Local SEO is $400 per month including care, with up to four hours total for care, research, implementation and reporting. We agree one priority based on evidence. This is not unlimited content, paid advertising or a promise of rankings.

Domain registration, hosting and any form-service or other third-party charges are separate. We identify those costs before work starts. A free hosting tier may suit a small static site, but availability and limits depend on the provider.

Compare the whole first year

For example, a $750 starter plus twelve months of optional $75 care totals $1,650 before domain, hosting or other third-party charges. The same starter with twelve months of $400 SEO including care totals $5,550; do not add another care fee.

A monthly website package from another provider can be a sensible option, including for a business that needs leads. Compare its setup fee, minimum term, included work, cancellation rules and ownership terms. A low upfront price alone does not tell you which offer costs less overall.

Know what you own and what changes cost

With Nimbus, you receive the site files after full payment and keep control of your domain. Monthly services are optional. These packages are static websites; they do not include a visual editing dashboard.

The deposit is half the build price. The balance is due after you approve the preview and before launch. A handoff and 30 days of corrections for defects in the agreed build are included.

A revision round means one consolidated list of feedback on the agreed work. New features or a changed brief are additional scope, quoted before work begins.

A repair may be enough

For an existing site with one clear problem, Nimbus offers a $300 focused fix covering one agreed task of up to three hours, including access checks, communication and verification. We confirm that it fits before you commit.

A new design does not guarantee search visibility or customers. Consider the actual problem first: your offer, content, contact path, search presence or website performance. A free focused review can help choose the next step.

Common questions

What is the least expensive Nimbus website?

The $350 single-page option is the smallest offer. It uses supplied copy and photos, includes one revision round and provides call and email links instead of a contact form. Domain, hosting and any third-party service charges are separate.

Do I have to pay Nimbus every month?

No. Care and SEO are optional. You receive the website files after full payment and keep control of your domain. Hosting and other third-party services may still have ongoing charges.

Does the $400 SEO plan also need $75 care?

No. Care is included in the $400 total and within the four-hour monthly work allowance.

Will a more expensive site rank better?

Price alone does not determine rankings. Relevant content, technical accessibility, competition and many other factors matter. Neither website package guarantees rankings or leads.

Can I edit the site myself?

You receive the files and can have another developer work on them. The packages do not include a visual content editor. Ask for a separate quote if that is a requirement.

Is my website actually findable?

Start with a site:yourdomain.com search, then confirm individual pages in Search Console. Search results are a useful spot check, but they are not a complete inventory of indexed pages.

Why your Google Business Profile beats your website

Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. Accurate business details and genuine reviews help customers assess you; Google does not publish fixed percentage weights or guarantee a position.

Why city pages usually backfire

A city page should answer a real local need with useful, distinct information. Near-duplicate pages can compete for the same intent, and pages made mainly to funnel similar searches can violate Google's doorway-abuse policy.
