Web design in Bradenton, for businesses that were here first.

Bradenton is an older business town than the master-planned communities east of it, and that shows up in its websites: a lot of good, established companies are running sites built years ago on platforms that make them hard to find. That is usually the fixable problem, not the design.

We work across Manatee County, and Bradenton is the part of it where the pattern is most consistent. The businesses are frequently older than the web — family trades, shops, restaurants, practices that have been on the same street for twenty years and got a website somewhere along the way because they needed one.

That site is often fine to look at. It is also often invisible: built on a builder that renders its content with JavaScript, missing page titles, one description repeated across every page, no sitemap, and a Google Business Profile filled in once and never touched. None of that is visible in a browser, which is exactly why it survives for years.

The specific thing that goes wrong here

A newer business in a newer development usually got a newer site, because it was built after the platforms got better at the basics. An established Bradenton business often has a site from an earlier generation of tools, and those tools shipped defaults that are actively harmful — a single shared page title, content that only appears after scripts run, image files straight off a camera.

The result is a company with twenty years of reputation losing search results to a competitor who opened last year, and no obvious reason why. It is rarely that the newer company is better. It is that the newer company's site can be read.

How to check your own site before calling anyone

Search Google for site:yourdomain.com as a quick spot check. The results are not a complete inventory, so confirm a missing page with Search Console's URL Inspection tool before diagnosing an indexing problem.

Then open your own site on your phone, on cell data rather than wifi, and count the seconds. Then look at the tab title — if every page says the same thing, or says "Home", search engines have nothing to distinguish them with.

There is a longer version of this in the guide on whether your website is findable. It takes about ten minutes and you do not need us to do it.

Bradenton and Lakewood Ranch are not the same market

They are twenty minutes apart and they behave differently. Lakewood Ranch is newer, more corporate, and its businesses tend to arrive with branding already decided. Bradenton has more independents, more trades, more places where the owner answers the phone — and, in the Village of the Arts and around the older downtown, a lot of very small businesses whose entire web presence is one page and a Facebook profile.

For most of those, the Google Business Profile matters more than the website does, and we will tell you that before selling you a website. The profile is what puts you in the map results; the site is what convinces someone once they get there. Both matter, but they do not matter equally, and they do not matter in the order most people assume.

Why this is the only city page on this site

Because we publish a guide arguing that templated town pages backfire, and we would rather be consistent than well-optimised.

This page exists because Search Console showed real Bradenton searches reaching a site with no Bradenton content — not because a spreadsheet had an empty row. There is no Sarasota, Palmetto, Venice or Parrish version of this page, and there will not be unless the same two conditions are met: real measured demand, and something true and specific to say about that market.

Common questions

Do you work with businesses in Bradenton?

Yes. Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, Sarasota and the rest of Manatee and Sarasota counties are the home market, and most work is done with people we can meet.

My Bradenton business has an old website. Rebuild or repair?

It depends on what is wrong. If the content is good and the platform is holding it back, that is a migration. If the site is three pages and none of them say what you do, that is a rebuild. We will tell you which after looking, and looking is free.

Will a new website put me at the top of Google in Bradenton?

Not on its own, and anyone promising that is guessing. Map results are driven mostly by your Google Business Profile, your proximity to the searcher and your reviews. A well-built site helps you rank for the searches below the map, and it is what makes the click turn into a phone call.
