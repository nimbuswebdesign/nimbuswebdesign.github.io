Web design for Bradenton businesses.

Nimbus is based in nearby Lakewood Ranch and serves businesses across Bradenton and Manatee County. We build websites and review existing ones for problems with search visibility, speed and the steps a customer takes to contact you.

The starting point is your existing site and the work you want more of. A shop with a public location needs clear directions and opening hours. A contractor who travels to customers needs useful service information, real project examples and a straightforward way to request an estimate.

An older website does not automatically need replacing. We look for specific problems first: pages Google cannot access, unclear service descriptions, slow mobile pages, broken contact forms or an outdated address. The platform name alone does not diagnose any of those.

Repair, rebuild or move the existing site?

Repair makes sense when the site describes the business well and the problems can be fixed within its current platform. A rebuild becomes worth discussing when customers cannot find the information they need or maintaining the site keeps getting in the way. The recommendation should explain what will change and why.

Moving a site also means protecting its existing URLs. We map old pages to relevant new ones, use permanent redirects where addresses change, and check the sitemap, contact links and forms. A fresh design is only one part of the job.

How to check your own site before calling anyone

Search Google for site:yourdomain.com as a quick spot check. The results are not a complete inventory, so confirm a missing page with Search Console's URL Inspection tool before diagnosing an indexing problem.

Open your site on your phone using cell data. Can you find the service, location and contact details without waiting or hunting? Check whether the tab titles describe each page. Google can render JavaScript; a JavaScript site needs an actual crawl or rendering check before anyone calls it invisible.

There is a longer version of this in the guide on whether your website is findable. It takes about ten minutes and you do not need us to do it.

A website and a Google Business Profile have different jobs

For a Bradenton customer, the map listing can answer immediate questions about your location, hours and reviews. Your website can explain the work in more detail and show whether it fits their needs. The business name, phone number and service information should agree across both.

Local map results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. A service-area page does not create a new office or guarantee a place in the map results. Nimbus's own base is Lakewood Ranch; serving Bradenton does not change that address.

What to send for a useful first review

Send your website address, the service you want more inquiries about, and the areas you actually cover. Mention anything that is already causing trouble, such as a form that fails or customers finding an old phone number. Do not send passwords in the contact form.

The free review gives you specific findings and a recommendation about what needs attention. A project quote depends on the pages, writing, features and migration work involved; those should be clear before you agree to a build.
