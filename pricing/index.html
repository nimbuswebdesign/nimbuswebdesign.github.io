Before we start

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, existing logo or branding, photos you have permission to use, and access to the accounts needed for launch. The Single-page package uses text you supply; Starter and Business include writing based on your facts. You review the content before publication.

What counts as a revision round?

One combined list of feedback on the agreed design and content. We make those changes, then send the updated preview. A new feature or a change of direction is quoted before additional work begins.

Are there any other costs?

Domain registration, hosting and any form-service or other provider fees are separate. We identify them before you commit. The packages cover one business and one language. Extra pages, a new logo, photography, online stores, booking systems and larger migrations need a separate quote.

Can I make changes myself?

You receive the site files after full payment and can have another developer work on them. These packages do not include a visual editing dashboard. Small text and photo updates can be handled through optional care; an editing dashboard can be quoted if you need one.

Will the website or SEO bring me customers?

The aim is to make your business easier to find and easier to contact. Basic search setup helps search engines discover and understand your pages. Rankings and inquiries also depend on competition, demand, your offer and reputation, so we do not promise a position or number of leads. Monthly SEO covers the agreed work; paid advertising and unlimited content are separate.

How do introductory pricing and payment work?

The prices shown apply to the package scope listed here. We confirm the price and schedule in your written quote before you commit. Half is due to start; the balance is due after you approve the preview, before launch. A later change to advertised prices does not change an accepted quote.

Not sure which option fits?

Tell us what you do and what you need the website to accomplish. If you already have a site, a free, focused review can help decide whether to keep it, fix it or rebuild.
