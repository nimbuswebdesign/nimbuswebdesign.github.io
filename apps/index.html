Keepwell: Swipe Photo Cleaner

Clean up your camera roll, one month at a time. Swipe left to delete and right to keep. Short sessions, clear choices.

Group look-alike shots and choose how similar counts. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days. Everything runs on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

How we build them

The same rules apply to the apps as to the websites: say plainly what the software does, and nothing it doesn't. Keepwell collects no data. Your photos are processed on your iPhone and are never uploaded, and the app has no account, no analytics and no ads. Purchases go through Apple, so we never see payment details.

Keepwell is coming to the App Store for iPhone. Questions about any of our apps go to apps@nimbuswebdesign.com.
