Keepwell · Privacy

Keepwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026. Applies to the Keepwell iOS app, published by Nimbus Web Design LLC ("we", "us").

Summary: Keepwell does not collect any data. Your photos and videos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. We have no account system, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. Keepwell does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share personal data or usage data. That's why the App Store privacy label for Keepwell reads "Data Not Collected".

Keepwell has no server and makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution software.

Your photos and videos

To let you review your library, Keepwell asks for access to your Photos library through the standard iOS permission prompt. You can grant full or limited access, and change it any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Keepwell.

  • Photos and videos are analyzed only on your device, using Apple's built-in frameworks (for example, PhotoKit and Vision), to show them to you and group similar shots.
  • Nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party. Keepwell has no server.
  • The little Keepwell remembers between sessions (such as the choices you've made and the running total of space freed) stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
  • Keepwell only works with photos and videos. It doesn't access your contacts, calendars, messages or email.

Deleting photos

Keepwell never deletes anything without the standard iOS confirmation. Deleted photos and videos go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days, after which iOS removes them permanently. See Apple's guide: Delete photos on your iPhone or iPad. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions sync to your other devices the same way as when you delete in the Photos app.

Purchases

Keepwell Pro is sold through the App Store, and Apple processes all payments and subscriptions. We never receive your name, email, payment card or billing details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active, so Pro features can be unlocked and restored. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Keepwell has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software, shows no ads, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Keepwell is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect no data, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on a child's behalf.

Your choices

You can revoke Photos access in iOS Settings at any time, or delete the app to remove all data Keepwell stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Keepwell handles data, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a future version collects any data, the App Store privacy label will be updated too.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email apps@nimbuswebdesign.com, or see the Keepwell support page. Terms of use: Apple's standard EULA.
