Keepwell Support

Keepwell helps you clean up your iPhone camera roll one month at a time. Swipe left to delete and right to keep, group look-alike shots with a similarity slider you control, and see how much space each session freed. Everything runs on your device.

Frequently asked questions

How does swiping work?

Pick a session (a month, On this day, or Screenshots, which uses the Screenshots album iOS keeps for you) and Keepwell shows its photos one at a time. Swipe left to mark a photo for deletion and right to keep it. Nothing is deleted while you swipe. When you're ready, review what you marked and confirm, and iOS shows its standard delete confirmation. Afterwards Keepwell shows how many items you cleared and how much space they took up.

Can I undo a swipe?

Yes, before you confirm the delete. Keepwell's Undo reverses your recent swipe choices, and you can also unmark items when you review them. Once a delete is confirmed, Undo can't bring the photos back. They're in Recently Deleted in the Photos app instead (see the next question).

I deleted a photo by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. Every photo or video you delete in Keepwell goes to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app. Open Photos, go to Collections, scroll to Utilities > Recently Deleted, unlock it with Face ID or Touch ID, select the items, then tap Recover. After 30 days, iOS removes them permanently and they can't be recovered. Apple's guide: Delete photos on your iPhone or iPad.

What does the How similar slider do?

In Duplicates & similar, Keepwell groups look-alike shots (bursts, near-duplicates, several tries at one photo) using on-device image analysis. The How similar slider decides what counts as a match: toward Exact, only near-identical shots are grouped; toward Loose, shots that are merely alike are grouped too. Groups update as you move the slider, without rescanning. Keepwell suggests a shot to keep in each group, and you choose.

What does the free version include?

Unlimited swiping and keeping, plus 50 deletions per day. The count resets at midnight, your iPhone's local time. Keepwell Pro removes the daily deletion limit.

What does Keepwell Pro cost?

Keepwell Pro is available as Monthly ($3.99 a month), Yearly ($14.99 a year) or Lifetime ($24.99, a one-time purchase that never renews). These are US prices. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy. Monthly and Yearly renew automatically until you cancel.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name (your Apple Account), tap Subscriptions, choose Keepwell and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Step-by-step: Cancel a subscription from Apple. The lifetime purchase is one-time and never renews.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Keepwell Pro, open Keepwell, go to Settings > Keepwell Pro, and tap Restore Purchases. The Keepwell Pro screen has the same button.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple, not from us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, choose the Keepwell purchase and follow the steps.

Does Keepwell upload my photos?

No. Photos and videos are analyzed only on your device using Apple's frameworks. Keepwell has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Does Keepwell touch my contacts or other data?

No. Keepwell only works with photos and videos in your Photos library.

Keepwell can't see all my photos.

You may have given limited access. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Keepwell and choose Full Access. Photos stored only in iCloud may need a network connection to load.
