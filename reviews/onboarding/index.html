Intake checklist

Give us Google Business Profile manager access

You stay the owner. A manager can reply to reviews but cannot delete the profile or remove you. You do this yourself; we never ask for your Google password.

  1. Sign in to the Google account that owns your Business Profile.
  2. Search Google for your business name, or open Google Maps, to bring up your profile's management panel.
  3. Open the three-dot menu (⋮) and choose Business Profile settings, then People and access.
  4. Select Add, enter the Nimbus email address we send you, and set the role to Manager (not Owner).
  5. Select Invite. We accept it and confirm by email. Repeat for each location.
  6. To remove us later, return to People and access and remove the Nimbus user.

Placeholder: The Nimbus manager-access email address is sent to you at signup; it is not published here. Google occasionally renames these menus; if a step looks different, look for "People and access" in your profile settings.

Review-request templates

Texts go only to homeowners who gave you written consent to receive texts. Everyone else gets email. Placeholders in braces are filled per customer.

{business}: review requests after your service. Questions? Call {phone}. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply.
