How this is different

Most small business sites are built to look finished, then handed over. Nothing checks whether search engines can read them, and the problems that keep a site invisible are exactly the ones that are invisible in a browser — a canonical pointing at the wrong page, a sitemap listing five URLs out of fifty, content that only appears once JavaScript runs.

Every site built here ships with an automated audit wired into the build. It checks titles, descriptions, canonicals, heading structure, image alt text, structured data, sitemap coverage in both directions, orphaned pages and broken internal links. If any of it fails, the build fails and the site does not deploy. That is the difference between a site that was checked once and a site that stays correct.

We also won't build you a hundred near-identical town pages, which is the most common thing sold as local SEO. It gets them clustered and ignored, and it drags down the pages that were genuinely good. Here's what happens when you do that, with measurements.

Is my website actually findable?

Search Google for site:yourdomain.com. If it returns one result, or none, search engines cannot see most of your website — and no amount of design work will fix that.

Why your Google Business Profile beats your website

Google Business Profile signals account for roughly a third of local pack ranking — more than the website contributes. A complete profile with steady reviews will outrank a better website attached to a neglected one.

Why city pages usually backfire

Near-identical pages get clustered by Google, which picks one and ignores the rest — so fifty city pages earn roughly the ranking value of one, while the site-wide quality signal drags down the pages that were genuinely good.
