Built for Tampa Bay roofers

Ask while the job is fresh

The best time to ask is the day the crew finishes and the yard is cleaned up. Requests go out automatically when you mark the job complete, so nobody has to remember.

Storm season without the backlog

After a storm, you may finish more jobs in a month than usual. Every one of those homeowners gets the same ask, and every review still gets a drafted reply.

Careful with complaints

Reviews about leaks, delays or bills over the quote never get an auto-reply. A draft waits for you, and it never admits fault or promises a refund on your behalf.

Common questions

Does AI write the replies?

AI drafts replies to your reviews. Positive-review replies can post automatically on rules you set. Replies to negative reviews (3 stars or lower) are never posted until you approve them.

Do you buy, filter or fake reviews?

No. We ask every homeowner the same way, and we do not gate requests by sentiment or offer incentives. That follows the FTC rule on consumer reviews and Google's review policies.

How do homeowners get asked?

By email after each job, and by text only to homeowners who gave written consent. Every text honors STOP, and we only text between 8am and 8pm.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. Cancel any time before your next billing date.

Consent & privacy

Texts and calls. We only send automated texts or calls to people who have given prior express written consent, as required by the federal TCPA and the Florida Telephone Solicitation Act (Fla. Stat. §501.059). Consent is always optional and is never a condition of a purchase or the free audit. Reply STOP to any text to opt out; HELP for help.

STOP / opt-out. You can opt out at any time: reply STOP to any text (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT), and we will confirm once and send nothing further. Reply HELP for help, or email us to revoke consent by any reasonable means.

What we record. When you consent, we keep the exact consent wording and its version, the time, the page, and your IP address as evidence of consent. We do not sell your information.

Email. Email consent is separate from text consent. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Review data. The audit uses public review information about your business. Questions: chris@nimbuswebdesign.com.
